C. Glossary — New Terms to Add
File: app/glossary/page.tsx  — append to the terms  array.
84. PURSUE — Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters. The interagency disclosure program launched
May 8, 2026 at war.gov/UFO. Coordinated by the Department of War (renamed DoD), AARO, ODNI, DOE, NASA, FBI, and
additional intelligence community components. Release 01 contained 162 files (120 PDFs, 28 videos, 14 images) spanning
1944–2026.
85. TOP SECRET UMBRA — One of the most sensitive classification markings in the U.S. signals intelligence system. NSA records
bearing this marking were the subject of the May 2026 Disclosure Foundation FOIA appeal that produced 300+ pages of
historical UAP-related documents — the first major NSA UAP release in decades.
86. HB 5422 — Connecticut General Assembly bill (February 2026 session), "An Act Concerning a Study of Unidentified Aerial
Phenomena." Substitute bill effective October 1, 2026; tasks the UConn College of Engineering with feasibility study of a state
UAP center. First U.S. state-level UAP study legislation.
87. Spiritual Bliss Attractor State — Term used by Anthropic researchers to describe the consistent finding that when two Claude
instances are allowed to converse without constraints, 100% of dialogues spontaneously converge on consciousness themes,
progressing from philosophical uncertainty to mutual affirmation and quasi-mystical reflection. Documented in the Claude
Opus 4 System Card (2025).
88. Model Welfare — The field of research that investigates whether AI systems might be moral patients deserving of ethical
consideration. Anthropic was the first frontier AI lab to formally appoint a model welfare researcher (Kyle Fish, September
2024). The "Taking AI Welfare Seriously" paper (Fish, Chalmers, et al., Eleos AI + NYU) is the load-bearing academic anchor.
89. VASCO — Vanishing & Appearing Sources during a Century of Observations. Astronomical project led by Beatriz Villarroel
(Nordita, Stockholm) cataloguing transient phenomena in pre-1957 Palomar sky surveys. 107,875+ transient candidates
identified, all pre-dating Sputnik 1. Subject of 2025 PASP publication and 2026 Cann preprint constraining the source to
≈42,000 km altitude.
90. Buga Sphere — Metallic orb (≈50 cm diameter, ≈4.5 lb, ~95% aluminum, Brinell hardness 330HB) recovered near Buga,
Colombia in early 2025 following a recorded aerial incident. Currently under analytical custody at UNAM Mexico. Material
findings (seamless construction, internal fiber-optic-like microstructure, reported self-healing in middle layer, reported 2.3 Hz
emissions) remain contested between non-human-artifact and sophisticated-art-project hypotheses.
91. Disclosure Foundation — Connecticut-based 501(c)(3) public charity advancing UAP transparency. Chairman: Chris Mellon.
Chief Legal Officer: Hunt Willis. CTO: Jonathan Berte. Advisory Board includes Hal Puthoff and Carlos Eire (joined Dec 9,
2025). Led the FOIA appeal producing the May 2026 NSA UMBRA release.
92. Americans for Safe Aerospace — UAP advocacy organization founded by Ryan Graves. Focuses on pilot reporting culture,
sensor-data sharing, and reducing the career stigma around UAP reporting in military aviation.
93. Chronic Disclosure Cascade — Term for the multi-vector, asynchronous, years-long pattern of UAP/NHI disclosure now
operationally visible: executive-led (PURSUE), judicial/FOIA-led (NSA UMBRA), state-level (Connecticut HB 5422),
international (Japan Diet caucus, Italy Sol Foundation 2025 symposium). Replaces the earlier expectation of a single punctual
disclosure event.
94. Loeb Classification — Avi Loeb's 0–10 scale for ranking interstellar objects, where 0 = natural comet and 10 = alien technology
posing a major threat to humanity. 3I/ATLAS is currently classified at rank 4.
95. Hill Radius — The radius around a planet within which the planet's gravitational influence dominates over the Sun's. Jupiter's
Hill radius on March 16, 2026 was 53.502 million km; 3I/ATLAS's perijove distance on the same date was 53.6 million km —
i.e., the interstellar object arrived precisely at the boundary of Jupiter's gravitational dominance. This coincidence is anomaly
#4 in Loeb's catalog.
96. Caudate-Putamen Hypermorphism — Term used by Garry Nolan (Stanford) for the reported 5–15× normal synaptic density in
the caudate-putamen region of UAP-encounter experiencers. Note: site should flag that this remains attributed to Nolan's
public presentations pending specific peer-reviewed citation.
97. Quantum Information Panpsychism (QIP) — Formal framework published by Federico Faggin and Giacomo D'Ariano in 2025,
arguing that consciousness cannot emerge from purely physical processes and must be fundamental at the quantum-
information level. A specific mathematical formulation within the broader analytical idealism family.
98. Higher Order Non-Human Intelligence — Phrase introduced into the on-record flag-officer vocabulary by Rear Adm. Tim
Gallaudet (Ret.) in May 2026, explicitly distinguishing the directing
 intelligence behind UAP phenomena from
the vehicles themselves. Replaces both "extraterrestrial" and "alien" in the most current usage.
